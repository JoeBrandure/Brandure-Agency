**Brandure wrote this comparison and is one of the agencies in it.** We've kept every entry to what each agency publishes on its own site or what independent write-ups report, with sources linked, so you can check it yourself.

**The short answer:** dozens of Dubai agencies now sell AI search services, but most add them to an SEO retainer. Fewer than one in four publish a price. If you want AI search as the main job, look for agencies that track each AI engine separately and do third-party citation work; if you want it bundled with SEO, the large Dubai SEO agencies cover it.

## How we compared them

Each agency is described on five points:

1. **AI search offering:** dedicated AEO/GEO service, or part of SEO.
2. **Published pricing:** listed on their site, or quote-only.
3. **Focus:** who they mainly serve.
4. **Arabic:** whether bilingual work is mentioned.
5. **Who AI names:** whether the agency was among the 20 most-named agencies in AI answers to the "best AEO / AI search agency" questions Brandure tracks across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Claude and Copilot (Searchable, 24–30 October 2026).

We didn't score or rank agencies on results we can't verify.

## What the comparison shows

- **Most "AEO" in the UAE is sold inside SEO.** That suits businesses that need Google rankings fixed first. It suits less well if AI engines are already recommending your competitors and you need that changed specifically.
- **Prices are rarely published.** Five of the ten agencies above list a price, and three of those are SEO package prices rather than AI search specifically. Ask every agency for a written scope and price before a call turns into a proposal.
- **No agency owns the category yet.** In the AI answers we track, the most-named agency appears in about 5% of answers. That's unusual: in most categories a few names dominate.

## How to choose between them

- **If you mainly need SEO, with AI search as a bonus:** a large SEO agency with GEO included (SEO Sherpa, Tenet, Global Media Insight).
- **If you need Arabic and English:** ask specifically how they handle each language, because AI engines treat them as separate markets (Arab SEO, AIIMS Group).
- **If AI engines are recommending competitors and you need that changed:** a specialist that reports each engine separately and earns third-party citations. That's the gap Brandure was set up for.

Whoever you speak to, ask for a baseline of your current AI visibility on named questions before you sign. ## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best AEO agency in Dubai?**
It depends on what you need. Large SEO agencies such as SEO Sherpa and Tenet include AI search in SEO retainers; specialists such as Brandure focus only on AI search and report each engine separately. No agency dominates AI answers yet.

**Which UAE agencies publish AEO prices?**
Tenet (AED 7,500–15,000 a month), Arab SEO (AI SEO from AED 5,500), SEO Tech Experts (SEO from AED 2,999), Zenerom (SEO from AED 3,750) and Brandure (AED 6,500 or 12,000 a month) publish prices. Most others quote on request.

**Is GEO the same as AEO?**
Yes, in practice. Both mean getting your business named and cited in AI-generated answers. Agencies in the UAE use either term.

**Do UAE AEO agencies work in Arabic?**
Some do. AIIMS Group and Arab SEO mention Arabic work; ask any agency how it measures Arabic and English answers separately.
