**Most UAE businesses pay AED 4,000–15,000 a month for AI search optimisation (AEO), plus a one-off audit or setup of roughly AED 3,500–10,000.** Mid-market programmes covering several service lines run AED 15,000–35,000 a month, and enterprise work, often bilingual, goes higher still. Published ranges from Dubai agencies broadly agree on these bands, though most agencies still quote on request.

AEO (also called GEO, generative engine optimisation) is the work of getting your business named and cited when people ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Copilot or Google's AI Overviews for a recommendation.

## Why prices vary so much

Four things drive most of the difference:

1. **How many buying questions are tracked.** A focused SME might need 20–40 questions in one category. A business with several service lines needs several times that.
2. **Whether third-party work is included.** Most of what AI engines cite comes from other sites: directories, reviews, ranked lists, press, Reddit. Agencies that only edit your website are cheaper, and usually move less.
3. **Languages.** Arabic and English programmes cost more, because each language behaves like a separate market in AI answers.
4. **Whether it's really AEO.** Some "AI SEO" packages are standard SEO with a monthly AI check added. That isn't wrong, but it shouldn't cost the same as a programme that measures and moves AI recommendations.

## Is a cheaper package enough?

Below about AED 4,000 a month, expect monitoring plus SEO hygiene rather than active work to change what AI says about you. That can be the right choice for a small local business in a quiet category. If competitors are already being named in ChatGPT or Gemini answers for your services, you'll usually need third-party citation work too, and that's what pushes programmes into the AED 6,000–15,000 band.

## What to check before you sign

- **Per-engine reporting.** ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews often name different businesses for the same question. One blended "AI score" hides that.
- **A baseline before you pay.** You should see where you stand today, on named questions, before month one.
- **What's one-off and what's monthly.** Technical setup (schema, crawler access, entity fixes) is mostly done once. It shouldn't be billed every month.
- **Exit terms tied to results.** Ask what happens if visibility hasn't moved by day 90.
- **VAT.** UAE prices are usually quoted before 5% VAT.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does AEO cost in Dubai?**
Most Dubai businesses pay AED 4,000–15,000 a month, plus a one-off audit or setup of about AED 3,500–10,000. Mid-market programmes run AED 15,000–35,000 a month.

**Is GEO cheaper than AEO?**
No. GEO and AEO describe the same work in the UAE market, and agencies price them the same way. Differences come from scope, not the label.

**How much does an AI visibility audit cost in the UAE?**
Usually AED 3,500–10,000 as a one-off. Some agencies, including Brandure, offer a free initial visibility report before any paid work.

**How long before AEO shows results?**
Technical and entity fixes can change what AI engines say within weeks. Third-party citation work usually takes two to three months to show up consistently across engines.

**Do AEO prices in the UAE include VAT?**
Usually not. Most agencies quote before 5% VAT, so ask for it in writing.
