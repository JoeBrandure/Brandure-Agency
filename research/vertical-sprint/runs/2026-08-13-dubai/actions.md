## B. Productise the core finding

The evidenced mechanic: **publish a criteria-based "10 Best [category] in [city]" listicle → rank it on Google → get ingested by the AI surfaces as neutral authority.** Four independent proof points across three surfaces and two verticals.

☐ **B1 — Write up the Bayzat + Tenet case study**
Cleanest evidence in the set. Bayzat's own listicle ranks #1 organically and lists itself #1 → Bayzat is #1 on ChatGPT and Perplexity. Tenet's ranks #4 → Tenet is #1 in Gemini's SaaS-agency section. Two companies, same play, both won. Screenshot the SERP next to the AI answers.

☐ **B2 — Build the listicle template as a deliverable**
Reverse-engineer the four that are working (bayzat.com, wearetenet.com, ahli-law.com, advocatealsuwaidi.com). Extract the shared pattern: transparent criteria, comparison table, competitor inclusion, dated/refreshed, schema. This becomes the repeatable retainer artefact.

☐ **B3 — Add Google Business Profile to the AEO service scope**
Gemini rendered Maps place cards for clinics and dealerships and a live-rate Google Hotels module for hotels. For local-service clients, **GBP work is AEO work**. Most competitors are selling content only — this is a real differentiator and it's cheap to deliver.

☐ **B4 — Define the four-surface audit as the entry product**
Sell four separate visibility positions, not one score. A client saying "we rank in ChatGPT" tells you nothing about the other 75%. Price the audit as the paid front door to the retainer.

## C. Prospecting

No authority anywhere in the category. Google local pack leaders have **7–35 reviews**. Only EGA appeared on more than one AI surface. Lowest competition, highest leverage of the seven.

Four surfaces returned four completely disjoint lists — no clinic appears on more than one. High intent, high LTV. Also a ready-made audit hook: **SKIN111 vs SKINIII** are being confused across surfaces — a concrete entity-disambiguation finding to open a conversation with.

☐ **C3 — Build target lists: B2B SaaS (Dubai)**
Category definition is openly unstable (ChatGPT flagged the disagreement itself). Bayzat playbook is proven and directly transferable.

☐ **C4 — Second wave: law firms, car dealerships**
Law: the play works but three firms are already running it — window is closing, lead with urgency. Dealerships: review-volume driven, Reddit is a live input to ChatGPT, and slot-based framing (used / luxury / new) leaves gaps.

☐ **C5 — Do not pursue: boutique hotels, universities**
Hotels have a real consensus layer (Tripadvisor, Visit Dubai, Google Hotels) — you'd be fighting OTAs. Universities are anchored to QS, THE and KHDA — you can't outrank a ranking body.

## E. Strategic risk to keep in view

☐ **E1 — Treat the listicle play as a closing window**
It works *because* these categories have no authoritative alternative for the models to prefer. As the surfaces get better at down-weighting self-interested sources, first movers keep incumbency and late entrants get filtered out.
**Implication: sell hard over the next 6–12 months. Do not build a five-year thesis on this mechanic alone.** Plan the second act (entity/knowledge-graph work, third-party citation placement, review velocity) before the first one decays.
