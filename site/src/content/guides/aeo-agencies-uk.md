**Brandure wrote this comparison and is one of the agencies in it.** Every entry sticks to what the agency publishes or what independent write-ups report, with sources linked, so you can check it yourself.

**The short answer:** most established UK SEO agencies now sell GEO, the UK's usual name for AEO, as part of organic search. A smaller group treats it as a separate service. Published prices range from about £600 a month for add-ons to £13,000+ for enterprise programmes. If AI engines are already recommending your competitors, look for an agency that reports each engine separately and earns third-party citations, not one that only edits your site.

## How we compared them

Each agency is described on five points:

1. **AI search offering:** standalone GEO/AEO, or part of SEO.
2. **Published pricing:** listed, or quote-only.
3. **Focus:** who they mainly serve.
4. **Proof:** whether a write-up cites a measurable AI search result.
5. **Who AI names:** whether the agency was among the 20 most-named agencies in AI answers to the "best AEO / AI search agency" questions Brandure tracks across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Claude and Copilot (Searchable, 24–30 October 2026).

We didn't score or rank agencies on results we can't verify.

## What the comparison shows

- **GEO is mostly sold inside SEO in the UK.** That works if you need Google rankings and AI visibility moved together. It's less suited to a business whose Google rankings are fine but whose competitors keep getting recommended by ChatGPT or AI Overviews.
- **Published prices are the exception.** Three of the nine agencies above list prices. Ask for a written scope and price early.
- **Proof is thin across the market.** Few agencies publish measured AI search results; most case studies report Google traffic. Ask for before-and-after AI visibility on named questions, per engine.
- **No agency dominates AI answers.** In the answers we track, the most-named agency appears in about 5% of them.

## How to choose between them

- **If you mainly need SEO, with GEO included:** an established UK SEO agency (The SEO Works, Exposure Ninja, Impression).
- **If you run a large or technically complex site:** an enterprise-focused team (SALT.agency, Found).
- **If you sell online:** an ecommerce specialist (ClickSlice).
- **If you're in a regulated sector:** ask how compliance review is handled and priced (MarGen publishes a regulated-sector rate).
- **If AI engines recommend competitors and you need that changed specifically:** a specialist that tracks each engine separately and earns third-party citations. That's the gap Brandure was set up for.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best GEO agency in the UK?**
It depends on what you need. Established SEO agencies such as The SEO Works and Exposure Ninja include GEO in organic search; enterprise teams such as SALT.agency and Found handle large sites; specialists such as Brandure focus only on AI search. No agency dominates AI answers yet.

**Which UK agencies publish GEO or AEO prices?**
Straight Up Search (add-ons from £595 a month), MarGen (from £2,950 a month) and Brandure (approx. £1,400 or £2,600 a month) publish prices. Most others quote on request.

**Is GEO the same as AEO?**
Yes, in practice. Both mean getting your business named and cited in AI-generated answers. UK agencies more often say GEO.

**How do I check whether an agency gets results in AI search?**
Ask for before-and-after AI visibility on named questions, reported per engine (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, AI Overviews), not just Google traffic.
