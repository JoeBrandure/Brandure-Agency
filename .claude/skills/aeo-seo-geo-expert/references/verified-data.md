## 1. Zero-click and AI Overview prevalence

| Claim | Figure | Source | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zero-click share of Google searches | ~65–68% | SparkToro / Similarweb clickstream | Jun 2026 |
| Zero-click, US desktop, 100+ MSV keywords | 58.5% | Semrush | 2024–25 |
| AI Overviews present on all queries | ~20% | SparkToro / Similarweb | Jan–Apr 2026 |
| AI Overviews present on *tracked* queries | ~48% (+58% YoY) | BrightEdge | Feb 2026 |
| Zero-click when AIO present vs absent | ~83% vs ~60% | Similarweb | 2026 |
| CTR decline when AIO present | 15%–89% across 12 studies | Amsive (700k kw) → DMG Media | 2025–26 |
| CTR decline, aggregate | ~34.5% | Ahrefs (300k kw) | Apr 2025, upd. Dec 2025 |
| Click on a cited source when AIO shows | **~1%** | Pew Research (68,879 tracked searches, 900 adults) | Jul 2025 |

**The two-number discipline:** ~20% is AIO prevalence across *all* queries; ~48%
is prevalence across a *tracked keyword set*. Both are quotable — always state
which. Zero-click (~65–68%) is a separate metric and must not be presented as
AIO prevalence.

**Most useful single number:** Pew's ~1%. It's the cleanest way to move a client
from a traffic mindset to a citation mindset.

## 5. llms.txt — do not sell this

| Finding | Source | Date |
|---|---|---|
| Not needed for Search, AI Overviews or AI Mode; grouped with content chunking, AI-specific rewriting and special schema as tactics that **don't** help | Google AI optimisation guide | updated 15 Jun 2026 |
| Google doesn't support it and isn't planning to | Gary Illyes, Search Central Live | Jul 2025 |
| Compared it to the keywords meta tag | John Mueller | 2025–26 |
| Adoption 10.13% of 300k domains | SE Ranking | 2026 |
| XGBoost citation-frequency model got **more accurate** when llms.txt was removed — added noise, not signal | SE Ranking | 2026 |
| 36,120 instances, 8.8× YoY growth, but ~97% receive zero AI requests | Originality.ai (3M+ sites) | Jun 2026 |
| 408 of 500M+ AI bot visits targeted llms.txt | Limy | 2026 |
| Perplexity and Claude do retrieve it; coding agents (Cursor, Copilot, Windsurf, Cline) depend on it | multiple | 2026 |

**Position:** ship it in half a day for dev-tool, API and documentation clients
where agent-driven use cases exist. Never invoice it as an AEO deliverable, never
let it displace earned-media work. If a competitor is selling llms.txt as an AEO
service, that's a differentiation opening — say it plainly with the SE Ranking
finding.

## 6. Content type and retrieval behaviour

- Highest leverage across engines: **original data and proprietary research**,
  then case studies and pricing pages. Top-of-funnel "what is / how to" content
  underperforms for AI-referred traffic — measure it on citation frequency, not
  sessions.
- Format most consistently cited: data-backed topic guides with verifiable
  statistics, structured headings, FAQ-style Q&A content and regular updates.
- Retrieval latency: **Perplexity** live-retrieves (hours after indexing);
  **AI Overviews** follow Google indexing timelines; **ChatGPT** static training
  base plus faster retrieval for commercial intent.
- Adobe Analytics, 2025 holiday: AI-referred retail traffic +693% YoY, converting
  **31% better**, **254% higher revenue per visit**. Good for the "fewer but
  better clicks" argument — caveat that it's retail-specific.

## 7. Reconciliation — where heuristics need correcting

State these plainly. Getting them right is a credibility asset, and several are
things a prospect can check on their phone.

| Heuristic claim | Correction | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| "AI Overviews appear in over half of Google searches" / "more than 50% of searches end with AI Overviews" (CD) | Conflates AIO prevalence with zero-click. AIO ≈20% of all queries; ~48% is a tracked-keyword sample. Zero-click is the ~65–68% figure. **Do not repeat the 50% claim** — easily checked, and wrong. | High |
| "AI systems mostly read raw HTML, so JS content can't be read" (CD) | Overstated for Google, which renders JS. Correct for ChatGPT/Perplexity/Claude crawlers, which largely don't execute JS. Reframe as: server-render citable content for the non-Google surfaces. | High |
| Schema markup as a primary AEO lever (CD) | Google's Jun 2026 guidance explicitly says special schema isn't needed for its AI features. Schema = hygiene for entity disambiguation, rich results, machine-readable authorship. Don't sell as growth. | High |
| "FAQs are quite literally gold" (CD) | FAQ *content structure* is genuinely high-leverage — keep it. FAQ *rich-result markup* was deprecated for most sites by Google in 2023. Separate the two. | High |
| "YouTube is the most cited domain in AI Overviews" (CD) | Was defensible; superseded. Reddit ≈40% of consolidated citations, well ahead. YouTube remains a real **Gemini-specific** lever. | High |
| "90% of founders said AEO was the biggest revenue opportunity in 2026" (CD) | Unsourced vendor stat. Never quote. | High |
| "Thousands of companies, millions in revenue" via Searchable (CD) | Marketing claim, no independent verification. Don't cite as market evidence. | High |
| llms.txt "cannot hurt" (CD) | Technically true, commercially misleading. See §5. | High |
| Searchable as the measurement answer (CD) | Fine as a client-facing dashboard. Needs a domain to instantiate a workspace, so not a category-discovery tool. Manual sampling remains the discovery instrument. | High |
| Google handles 13–14bn searches/day (CD, citing SparkToro) | Broadly consistent with public estimates. Fine to use with attribution. | Medium |
| "60% of searches end without a click" (CD) | Close to, and slightly conservative against, the ~65–68% verified figure. Safe to use; the verified number is stronger. | High |
| "There is no Search Console for LLMs" (CD) | Partly superseded. GSC Generative AI report and Bing AI Performance now exist, for Google/Bing surfaces and own site only. Still nothing first-party for ChatGPT, Perplexity or Claude. See §8. | High |
| Bing report: "Copilot is the ceiling" (LS) | Slightly narrow. Covers Copilot, Bing AI summaries and select partner integrations, and expanded in Jun 2026. Still excludes ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AIO. | High |
| Share of voice = "% of responses that mention your brand vs competitors" (LS) | Ambiguous. Demo numbers (36% visibility, 11.2% SoV) imply share of total brand mentions. Confirm in-tool before quoting. | Medium |
| "You can't tally tone" — sentiment can't be done by hand (LS) | Overstated. Tone can be coded on a rubric at small n; the tool's edge is scale and consistency. Its 0–100 score has no disclosed method. | Medium |

**Pattern worth noting:** CD's *frameworks* survive scrutiny almost entirely
intact. His *prevalence figures* are where the errors cluster, and they skew in
the direction that makes the problem look more urgent and his tool more
necessary. Use the frameworks freely; verify every number.
