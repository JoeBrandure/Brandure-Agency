**Most UK businesses pay £2,000–5,000 a month for AI search optimisation (AEO), plus a one-off audit or setup fee of roughly £1,500–5,000.** Starter packages begin around £800 a month. Competitive B2B or multi-service programmes run £5,000–10,000 a month, and enterprise work starts around £10,000. Anything sold as full AEO for well under £1,500 a month is usually SEO with an AI check added.

AEO (often called GEO, generative engine optimisation, in the UK) is the work of getting your business named and cited when people ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Copilot or Google's AI Overviews for a recommendation.

## UK AEO prices at a glance (2026)

| What you're buying | Typical UK price | What it should include |
|---|---|---|
| One-off audit | £1,500–5,000 | How AI engines describe you and your competitors, which sources they cite, crawler access, entity problems, and a prioritised plan |

| Enterprise | £10,000–20,000+ / month | Multiple countries or languages, dedicated strategists, large-scale content and PR |

## Why UK prices vary so much

1. **How many buying questions are tracked.** One category with 20–40 questions costs far less than several service lines across regions.
2. **Third-party work.** Most AI citations come from sites other than yours: review platforms, directories, ranked lists, press, Reddit. Earning those placements is the expensive, effective part.
3. **Regulated sectors.** Finance, legal and health typically cost 25–40% more for the same scope, because content needs compliance review ([MarGen](https://www.margen.net/aeo-agency-pricing-uk/)).
4. **Whether it's really AEO.** Some packages are standard SEO renamed. Ask what changes in AI answers, not just in Google rankings.

## Is a starter package enough?

For a local business in a low-competition category, a £800–1,500 starter package that fixes crawler access, schema and key pages can be enough. If competitors are already being recommended in ChatGPT or AI Overviews for your services, you'll usually need third-party citation work, which is what the £2,000+ programmes pay for.

## What to check before you sign

- **Per-engine reporting.** ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and AI Overviews often name different businesses for the same question.
- **A baseline first.** See where you stand on named questions before month one.
- **Setup vs monthly.** Schema, crawler access and entity fixes are mostly one-off work.
- **Exit terms.** Ask what happens if visibility hasn't moved by day 90.
- **VAT.** Most UK prices exclude 20% VAT.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does AEO cost per month in the UK?**
Most UK businesses pay £2,000–5,000 a month. Starter packages begin around £800, and competitive multi-service programmes run £5,000–10,000.

**How much does a GEO or AEO audit cost in the UK?**
Usually £1,500–5,000 as a one-off. Some agencies credit the audit against the first month, and some, including Brandure, offer a free initial visibility report.

**Is GEO more expensive than AEO?**
Agencies use the terms interchangeably in the UK. Price follows scope (how many questions, markets and placements), not the label.

**How long before AEO shows results?**
Technical and entity fixes can show within weeks. Third-party citation work usually takes two to three months to show consistently across engines.

**Do UK AEO prices include VAT?**
Usually not. Most agencies quote excluding 20% VAT.
