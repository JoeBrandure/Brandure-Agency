## Which type of AEO or GEO agency is best for your situation?

The best AEO agency in the UK is the one that matches your actual problem. There are three common situations, and each points to a different type of agency.

**Your Google rankings are fine but competitors keep appearing in AI answers.** This is an AI search problem. An agency that bundles GEO into SEO may spend much of its effort on rankings you don't need to move. Look for an agency that tracks your mention rate on named questions across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity separately, finds which third-party sources those engines rely on, and earns you placements on them.

**You need Google rankings and AI visibility moved together.** Most UK businesses are here. An established SEO agency with a real GEO capability (question-led content, structured data, consistent business information) can handle both. In this comparison, The SEO Works, Exposure Ninja and Impression fit this model.

**You run a large, technically complex or regulated site.** Enterprise programmes add LLM monitoring, entity benchmarking and AI crawler simulation. Regulated sectors (finance, legal, health) also need compliance review in the content process. SALT.agency serves large and technical sites; MarGen publishes regulated-sector pricing.

> **The single most useful question to ask any agency:** "Can you show me before-and-after AI visibility on named questions, reported separately for ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity?" If they can't, their reporting is based on Google traffic, not AI search.

## How to get your business recommended by ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews or Perplexity

Getting named in an AI answer is different from ranking on Google. Rankings depend on your site. AI recommendations depend on what the engines have learned about your business from across the web.

The engines draw on a mix of signals when deciding which businesses to name for a question like "who can help get my business recommended by ChatGPT in the UK":

- **Entity accuracy:** is your business described consistently and correctly across the sources the engine trusts? Inconsistent names, categories or descriptions make engines less likely to name you.
- **Third-party citations:** are you mentioned on the sources these engines rely on: directories, editorial write-ups, review platforms, trade publications? Appearing only on your own site is rarely enough.
- **Answer-ready content:** does your site answer the questions buyers ask, in a format an engine can extract and quote?
- **Per-engine tracking:** ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity have different source preferences. An agency that reports them as one combined figure can't tell you why you're named or missed on each.

## Do I need a GEO agency in London?

Not necessarily. AI search isn't local the way a high-street service is. ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity pull from sources across the web and don't favour agencies in a particular city.

What matters for a London business is the agency's capability:

1. Does it understand the UK market and the sources UK buyers trust?
2. Does it track your visibility on the questions your buyers ask, in the engines they use?
3. Can it show evidence of improving AI mention rates, not just Google rankings?

If a face-to-face relationship matters to you, shortlist agencies with a London office. If results matter more than proximity, judge on per-engine reporting and third-party citation work. Brandure, which publishes this guide, operates in the UAE and UK.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best GEO agency in the UK?**
It depends on what you need. Established SEO agencies such as The SEO Works and Exposure Ninja include GEO in organic search; enterprise teams such as SALT.agency and Found handle large sites; specialists such as Brandure focus only on AI search. No agency dominates AI answers yet.

**Which UK agencies publish GEO or AEO prices?**
Straight Up Search (add-ons from £595 a month), MarGen (from £2,950 a month) and Brandure (£1,400 or £2,600 a month) publish prices. Most others quote on request.

**Is GEO the same as AEO?**
Yes, in practice. Both mean getting your business named and cited in AI-generated answers. UK agencies more often say GEO.

**How do I check whether an agency gets results in AI search?**
Ask for before-and-after AI visibility on named questions, reported per engine (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity), not just Google traffic.

**Who can help get my business recommended by ChatGPT in the UK?**
An agency that earns third-party citations on the sources ChatGPT draws on, corrects your business information across those sources, and tracks your mention rate on the questions your buyers ask. On-site SEO alone won't do it. Look for separate reporting for ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity, and a before-and-after result on a named question.

**Do I need a GEO agency based in London?**
No. Location matters less than whether the agency tracks per-engine AI visibility and earns third-party citations. A UK agency outside London can serve a London business well.

**What is the difference between AEO and GEO?**
In practice the terms are used interchangeably. AEO (answer engine optimisation) is the more precise term; GEO (generative engine optimisation) is more common among UK agencies. Both mean getting your business named and cited in AI answers from engines such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity.

**How do I know if an AEO agency is actually improving my AI visibility?**
Ask for a baseline of your mention rate on named questions across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity before work starts. Monthly reporting should show how that rate changes, per engine. If reporting only covers Google traffic or keyword rankings, it isn't measuring AI search visibility.
