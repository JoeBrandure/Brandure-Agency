# Commercial

Packaging, pitching, promise guardrails and Brandure's own programme. Guidelines
— adapt to the client and the market.

---

## Deliverable shape

**Entry product: multi-surface visibility audit.** Priced as the paid front door
to a retainer. Sold as separate visibility positions per surface, never one
blended score — cross-engine overlap is 11–12%, so a single score is
actively misleading.

**Live demo, no deck.** Run the client's own category prompt across all four
surfaces in the meeting. For a law firm, show a competitor's own blog quoted back
as neutral truth — and in Gemini's case, cited as the source for the entry about
*them*. Two minutes, and it sells the retainer without slides.

**Delivery platform:** Searchable's agency plan, white-labelled so the workspace
presents as Brandure's own system. The free visibility report is the hook; the
tracked workspace is the product.

**Retainer artefacts:**
- Monthly drift report (W9) — the recurring product
- Earned placement pipeline (W7) — the main value driver
- Content cluster production (W6)
- Entity remediation (W4)
- GBP programme (W8) where local intent exists

**Repeatable IP:** the criteria-based category listicle template, reverse
engineered from the working examples in the Dubai scan — transparent criteria,
comparison table, competitor inclusion, dated and refreshed, named author,
schema.

## Positioning arguments that hold up

- **"AI Overview coverage is thin here."** It fired on 1 of 7 commercial "Best X
  in Dubai" queries in the UAE. Calibrates expectations and positions Brandure as
  the party with local data rather than vendor hype.
- **"Ranking no longer buys citation."** Top-10 organic share of AIO citations
  fell from ~76% to 17–38% in under a year. This is why AEO is a distinct
  discipline and not an SEO upsell.
- **"You're invisible on the surfaces you're not measuring."** 11–12% overlap,
  up to 615× volume variance for the same brand.
- **"Placement is the product, on-site is hygiene."** The majority of non-paid AI
  citations come from earned media, not brand-owned domains. Sets the scope
  expectation correctly and pre-empts "can't we just fix the website."
- **"A snapshot isn't a programme."** The free visibility report shows the gap
  once. The retainer is the tracked loop — act on the Opportunities tab, ship,
  re-measure — and the proof is consistent movement in visibility, share of
  voice, citations and sentiment, per engine, over time.
- **"GBP work is AEO work."** For local services. Unglamorous, cheap, and most
  competitors are selling content only.

## Promise guardrails

- Never promise a position in an AI answer. Promise process, measurement and
  citation-surface expansion.
- Never promise meaningful movement in under ~4 months — while sequencing the
  faster levers (Perplexity, GBP, entity fixes) first to demonstrate motion.
- State the closing-window risk in writing. It's a reason to act now, not
  something to conceal.
- Never present signed-in or n=1 sampling as evidence.
- Never quote a vendor prevalence stat without independent corroboration.
- When a client asks for "GEO" as a separate service, explain it's the same stack
  and decline to double-bill.
- Brandure has no client results yet. Say so. Its own programme is the case study.

## Brandure's own programme

Its own visibility *is* the case study, so this sequence matters commercially,
not just internally.

1. **Astro static build** — already correct. Server-rendered HTML is what the
   non-Google engines can actually read (heuristic D1).
2. **Technical floor** — Organisation schema, named-author Article schema,
   sitemap to Google **and** Bing, bot access verified for
   GPTBot/ClaudeBot/PerplexityBot. Confirm GSC Generative AI and Bing AI
   Performance are visible (W11) so its own first-party history starts now.
3. **The vertical research index** — the citable original-research asset.
   Simultaneously the lead magnet, the digital-PR hook and the topical-authority
   anchor. Highest-leverage single artefact in the plan.
4. **Cluster around it** — per-surface citation breakdowns, the listicle-mechanic
   case study, vertical-specific guides, comparison pages.
5. **Entity footprint** — Crunchbase, LinkedIn company page, Google Business
   Profile, relevant UAE trade directories, consistent description everywhere.
6. **Disclosed community participation** where UAE founders and marketers ask
   about AI visibility.
7. **Baseline its own tracked prompt set** (W1/W2) at the start, so the
   before/after is provable and becomes the first case study.
