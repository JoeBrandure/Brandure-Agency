# AEO pricing benchmarks — Searchable, UAE, UK

Date: 2026-08-30

Market scan of what AEO work is being sold for, run to inform the pricing
decision recorded as blocked in `STATE.md`. Covers three questions: what
Searchable charges an agency, what AEO specialists charge in the UAE and the
UK, and what agencies bundling AEO with SEO and web work charge.

## Credibility note — read this before using any figure below

**Every figure here is second-hand.** The session's network policy blocks
direct page fetching entirely, so nothing on this page was read from the
source site. All of it comes from search-engine summaries of those pages.
That means:

- Figures may be stale, may be from a page section that qualifies them, and
  may have been paraphrased into a precision they do not have.
- Nothing here has been seen in the context of its own small print — minimum
  terms, what counts as a "prompt", whether VAT or setup is included.
- Two of the sources are AEO agencies writing about AEO agency pricing. Those
  are marketing pages with a commercial interest in the number they quote.

**Treat these as bands, not as prices.** Any figure that ends up load-bearing
in a Brandure rate card must be re-checked against the primary page first.

Currency: AED is pegged at 3.6725 to the USD. GBP/AED is taken here at
approximately 4.7, which floats — conversions below are indicative only.

## 1. Searchable

Two separate commercial routes, and the search results conflate them. The
distinction matters more than any single number.

### List pricing

| Plan | Price | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $50/mo | 1 domain, 50 prompts |
| Professional | $125/mo | Unlimited domains, 200 prompts |
| Scale | $400/mo | Unlimited domains, 2,000 prompts |

Three tiers, confirmed across several independent review sites. A promotional
30% off annual was running at the time of the scan.

### The affiliate route — figures published

30% recurring commission on every referred customer, for as long as they stay,
rising to 40% at 10 active referrals. This is the route documented at
`searchable.com/affiliates`.

### The agency partner route — figures NOT published

The agency programme at `searchable.com/solutions/agencies` describes
white-labelling (custom logo, colours, subdomain and report covers, with
Searchable's name removed from client-facing surfaces), discounted pricing
across all plans, a single agency view across client accounts, and a listing
in a public agency directory.

**No discount percentage or agency price is published.** It is quoted on
application. The 30/40% figures above are the affiliate programme and should
not be assumed to apply here.

### What this means for Brandure

**The commission is not the point and should not influence the decision.**
30% of a $125/month Professional plan is $37.50 a month. At the four-clients-a-
quarter cap the site already states, the entire commission line is a rounding
error against a single retainer. Anyone selecting a platform on commission at
this scale is optimising the wrong number.

**The white-label and the directory listing are the point.** The listing is a
lead source aimed at exactly the buyer Brandure wants, and white-labelling is
what stops the client concluding they could licence the tool themselves for
$125 and skip the agency.

**Platform cost is immaterial to the pricing model.** Even at list, Scale is
about AED 1,470/month against a retainer priced in five figures. Cost-to-serve
is Joe's hours, which is the figure that is still unknown.

## 2. UAE — AEO specialists

Published tiers, Dubai:

| Tier | Monthly | Typical scope as described |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | AED 3,000–6,000 | English only, ~10 pages/month, basic schema, citation audit across 2–3 platforms |
| Growth | AED 7,000–14,000 | Adds Arabic, expanded schema, competitive citation monitoring, 15–25 assets/month |
| Enterprise | AED 18,000+ | — |

Named agencies:

- **Hikmah AI** — from AED 6,000/month, six-month minimum.
- **NERDSEY** — AED 8,000/month for the "Traffic Accelerator" tier, month to
  month, senior-only team.

Note what the scope descriptions are actually selling: pages produced and
schema applied. Volume of content, not position in an answer. That is the
prevailing shape of the UAE offer.

## 3. UK — AEO and GEO specialists

| Band | Monthly |
|---|---|
| Entry / monitoring only | £99–£2,500 |
| Mid-market (most common) | £2,000–£8,000 |
| Enterprise | £10,000–£25,000+ |

The most common productised done-for-you band is quoted at £600–£3,000/month,
with custom enterprise work at £4,000–£10,000.

Named agencies with published prices:

- **AEO-REX** — £99/month, monitoring only.
- **Bamsh Digital** — £799/month + VAT, single tier, rolling monthly, SEO and
  AEO bundled.
- **NeuralAdX** — up to £10,000/month at the Elite tier, sold with
  citation-volume guarantees.

Two structural facts worth more than the numbers:

- **Most agencies do not publish.** Reported at roughly one third publishing,
  two thirds quoting on request. Price opacity is the norm in this category.
- **A separate one-off setup or audit fee of £1,500–£5,000 is standard** on
  top of the retainer, covering the initial audit and entity foundation work.

## 4. Bundled — AEO with SEO, and with web

- AEO/GEO as an **add-on to an existing SEO retainer**: £720–£2,400/month.
- AEO/GEO as a **dedicated programme**: £1,500–£18,000+/month.
- Guidance in circulation is to treat AEO as **15–25% of total SEO budget**
  rather than buying it separately, and that adding AEO raises traditional SEO
  cost by **20–50%**.
- UK full-service retainers across multiple channels: £3,500–£16,750/month.
  Core single-service retainers: £1,250–£3,500/month.
- Dubai SEO retainers: AED 3,000–8,000/month for SME, AED 2,500–6,000 for
  monthly management, AED 6,500+ where AI/AEO/GEO is included, and AED
  15,000–40,000+ in competitive verticals (legal, medical, real estate).

## 6. Where Brandure's placeholders currently sit

The site carries these, explicitly flagged as placeholders for layout:

| | Placeholder | Approx |
|---|---|---|
| The visibility audit | AED 9,500 one-off | ~$2,590 / ~£2,020 |
| The ongoing retainer | from AED 14,000/month | ~$3,810 / ~£2,980 |

**The audit is well placed.** AED 9,500 sits mid-band against the $1,500–5,000
common range and roughly half the Avante comparable, which is the closest
match on scope. There is room above it, not below.

**The retainer entry point is the exposed number.** AED 14,000 is the *ceiling*
of the published Dubai Growth band and the point at which the local market
starts calling a plan enterprise. It is a premium price being asked by an
agency with no published case studies and no named clients.

Against the UK it reads differently — £2,980 is mid-market there, unremarkable.

So the placeholder is not wrong, it is **market-specific**, and nothing on the
site currently says which market it is for. That is a real gap: the same number
is aggressive in Dubai and ordinary in London.

## 7. What this does and does not settle

**It does not unblock pricing.** `STATE.md` blocks pricing on ICP and on real
delivery hours per engagement, and neither is answered by knowing what other
people charge. A market band tells you what a buyer will tolerate. It says
nothing about whether the work is profitable at that number for one person
with evenings, which is the binding constraint.

**What it does settle:**

1. The audit-then-retainer shape is the market-standard structure, not an
   invention. A separate one-off fee of £1,500–5,000 alongside a retainer is
   normal practice.
2. Platform cost is not a pricing input. It is under 5% of a single retainer
   at list price.
3. Searchable's commission is not a revenue line worth modelling at this
   scale. The white-label and the directory listing are the reasons to pursue
   partner access, and both are already on the `STATE.md` blocked list for
   other reasons.
4. Price opacity is the category norm. Publishing a price at all is a
   differentiator, and consistent with the positioning already on the site.

**Still needed before a rate card exists:**

- Delivery hours from running `brandure-agency-measure` end to end once.
- Which market the price is denominated for, since AED 14,000 means two
  different things in Dubai and London.
- Whether the audit fee is credited against the first retainer invoice. Market
  practice is split and the site currently says the audit commits you to
  nothing, which is a stronger claim than most competitors make.
