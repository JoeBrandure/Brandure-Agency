# ANALYSIS

## 1. The core finding: self-published listicles are grounding the AI layer

The most cited source in this entire study is not a directory, a regulator or a publisher. It is **brands writing "10 Best X in Dubai" posts about their own category and ranking themselves first.**

| Self-published listicle | Ranks on Google | Result in AI answers |
|---|---|---|
| ahli-law.com | — | Cited on **every line** of Perplexity's law-firm answer; firm placed **#1** |
| advocatealsuwaidi.com | **Page 1 organic** | Grounds the **majority** of Gemini's law-firm answer, incl. its competitors |
| bayzat.com | **#1 organic** | Bayzat is **#1 on ChatGPT and Perplexity** |
| wearetenet.com | **#4 organic** | Tenet is **#1** in Gemini's SaaS-agency section |

Four independent instances, three surfaces, two verticals. Gemini even *names* Chambers & Partners and Legal 500 as its authority, then grounds the answer on a law firm's blog instead.

**This is the Brandure product.** Not "optimise for AI" as an abstraction — a specific, evidenced, repeatable mechanic: publish the criteria-based category listicle, rank it, get ingested as the neutral source.

**The honest risk:** this is a land-grab with a closing window. It works because these categories currently have no authoritative alternative for the models to prefer. As the surfaces get better at down-weighting self-interested sources, first-movers keep the incumbency and late entrants get filtered. That argues for selling this hard in the next 6–12 months, not building a five-year thesis on it.

## 2. Cross-surface overlap is far lower than anyone assumes

Brand overlap across the four surfaces, same prompt, same day:

| Category | Agreement | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Universities | **High** | ChatGPT/Gemini both lead with Birmingham; QS + THE anchor everything |
| Boutique hotels | Moderate | Tripadvisor/Visit Dubai form a consensus layer |
| Car dealerships | Moderate | ChatGPT↔Gemini share 3 brands; Perplexity is the outlier |
| Law firms | Low | 3 of 9 shared between ChatGPT and Perplexity |
| B2B SaaS | Low | Bayzat wins two surfaces, is **absent** from the third |
| Aesthetic clinics | **Zero** | Four surfaces, four disjoint lists, no brand on more than one |
| Industrial manufacturers | **Near-zero** | Only EGA appears twice across three surfaces |

**Commercial implication:** a client who says "we rank in ChatGPT" is telling you almost nothing about the other 75% of the surface area. Sell the audit as four separate visibility positions, not one score. Aesthetic clinics and industrial manufacturers are demonstrably unowned by anyone right now.

## 3. Each surface has a different lever — this is what makes it a service, not a tool

| Surface | What actually drives the answer | Lever |
|---|---|---|
| **ChatGPT** | Cited listicles + Google review volume/rating; Reddit in some verticals | Content + citation placement + review velocity |
| **Perplexity** | Self-published listicles + a separate Places module | Listicle ranking + Google Business Profile |
| **Gemini** | Google Maps place cards, Google Hotels feed, self-published listicles | **Google Business Profile and product feeds first**, content second |
| **Google Search** | Organic listicle ranking + local pack + ads | Classic SEO + GBP |

Gemini is effectively a Google Business Profile surface for local categories — it rendered Maps cards for clinics and dealerships and a live-rate booking module for hotels. **For any local-services client, GBP work is AEO work.** That is a useful, unglamorous thing to be able to say in a pitch, and it is not what most "AEO agencies" are selling.

## 4. Vertical prioritisation for Brandure's own prospecting

**Go first — no incumbent, cheap to win:**
1. **Industrial manufacturers** — no authority anywhere, local pack has 7–35 reviews, near-zero cross-surface overlap. Highest-leverage, lowest-competition category in the set.
2. **Aesthetic clinics** — zero consensus across four surfaces, high commercial intent, high customer LTV, and an obvious entity-disambiguation problem (SKIN111 vs SKINIII) that is a concrete, demonstrable audit finding.
3. **B2B SaaS** — category definition is openly unstable (ChatGPT said so itself); the Bayzat playbook is proven and repeatable here.

**Go second — winnable but contested:**
4. **Law firms** — the listicle play demonstrably works, but three firms are already running it. Still open, getting less so.
5. **Car dealerships** — review-volume driven, Reddit is a live input, and slot-based framing (used vs luxury vs new) leaves gaps.

**Deprioritise:**
6. **Boutique hotels** — a real consensus layer exists (Tripadvisor, Visit Dubai, Google Hotels). You would be fighting OTAs on their own turf.
7. **Universities** — anchored to QS, THE and KHDA. Institutional, slow, and you cannot outrank a ranking body.

## 5. Sharpest single pitch artefact

For a law firm or a SaaS company: run this exact prompt live on all four surfaces in the meeting. Show them a competitor's own blog post being quoted back as neutral truth — and, in Gemini's law-firm answer, being quoted as the source **for the entry about them**. That is a two-minute demo that sells the retainer without a deck.

## ⚠ Methodology caveats — read before using externally

1. **Gemini and Google Search were run signed in as Joe.** The Claude extension could not attach to the incognito window and opened its own on the default Chrome profile. ChatGPT and Perplexity were logged out and clean. Gemini and Google results are subject to account history and personalisation. Geo signal was correctly Dubai throughout (ChatGPT geolocated to Warsan; Gemini's Maps modules centred on Dubai), so the market is right — the contamination is account history, not location. **Re-run those 14 before showing this to a client.**
2. **Single run per prompt.** No repeat sampling, so run-to-run variance is not measured. Some of the "zero overlap" findings would tighten with n=3.
3. **Free/default model tiers** — ChatGPT logged-out auto, Gemini Flash, Perplexity free. Paid tiers and reasoning models may ground differently.
4. **One prompt per category.** Findings describe `Best [category] in Dubai` specifically, not the full query space.
5. **Point-in-time, 13 Aug 2026.** These answers move.
