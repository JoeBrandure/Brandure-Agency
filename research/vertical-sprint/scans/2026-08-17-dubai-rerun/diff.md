## New

### The self-citation mechanic does not transfer to Google AI Overviews

**Bayzat ranks first organically for B2B SaaS and does not appear in the AI
Overview at all.** The AIO cites F6S, Creative Marketing UAE, Yello UAE and DXB
Start instead.

The mechanic is evidenced on **ChatGPT and Perplexity, not on AIO.** This bounds
the core commercial finding: publishing a self-ranking listicle and ranking it
organically is demonstrated to win answer-engine citations, and is demonstrated
*not* to win a Google AI Overview citation in the one case observed. Any pitch
built on the mechanic should scope it to the surfaces where it is evidenced.

### pesa.co grounds the universities AI Overview

**Five of seven inline citations** in Google's universities AI Overview come
from `pesa.co` — a low-authority aggregator — in a category otherwise anchored
to QS and Times Higher Education.

Read alongside the listicle finding, this points the same way: the grounding
layer is more contestable than the named authorities suggest, and it is not
always the authoritative source doing the work.

## What this diff does not establish

Every item above is a comparison of two single runs four days apart, on
different Gemini model tiers. **Two headline findings moved between them.** The
correct reading is not that the 17 August values are the true ones — it is that
neither run's values are yet known to be stable.

This is the case for action A3 (re-run at n=3), and it is now stronger than when
A3 was written.
